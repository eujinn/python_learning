人生最大的成就，是從失敗中站立起來。
Life's truest masterpiece is the courage to rise gracefully after every fall.

---

真正的愛心，是照顧好自己的這顆心。
The most profound act of love begins with tending gently to your own heart.

---

有智慧才能分辨善惡邪正；有謙虛才能建立美滿人生。
Wisdom is the light that parts the shadows of right and wrong; humility is the soil from which a beautiful life blooms.

---

時間可以造就人格，成就事業，也可以儲積功德。
Time is the quiet sculptor of character, the builder of dreams, and the gatherer of grace.

---

培養好自己的氣質，不要爭面子，爭來的是假的，養來的才是真的。
Nurture the quiet elegance of your soul rather than chasing hollow prestige. What is grasped is fleeting; what is cultivated endures.

---

有心就有福，有願就有力。
A mindful heart invites profound blessings; a pure vow awakens boundless strength.

---

大錯誤容易反省，小習氣不易除掉。
Great missteps awaken swift remorse, yet it is the subtle shadows of old habits that are hardest to wash away.

---

多做多得，少做多失。
To pour yourself into the world is to be filled; to hold back is to slowly empty your spirit.

---

一個人不怕錯，就怕不改過；改過並不難。
Fear not the stumbling blocks of error, but the stillness of an unbending will. To mend a fault is simply to choose the light.

---

不經一事不長一智。
Every trial weathered is a seed of wisdom sown.

---

吃苦了苦，苦盡甘來；享福了福，福盡悲來。
To embrace sorrow is to watch it dissolve into dawn; to carelessly consume joy is to invite the dusk.

---

人因自覺而成長，因自滿而墮落。
We blossom in the gentle light of self-awareness, and wither in the shadows of our own complacency.

---

不要小看自己，因為人有無限的可能。
Never doubt the vastness within you; the human spirit is an ocean of infinite shores.

---

信心、毅力、勇氣三者具備，則天下沒有做不成的事。
Anchor yourself in faith, walk with endurance, and breathe in courage—no mountain is too high for such a spirit.

---

願要大，志要堅，氣要柔，心要細。
Let your vision be vast and your resolve unyielding, yet keep your spirit as gentle as a breeze and your mind as clear as still water.

---

水滴雖微，漸盈大氣。
A single drop of water whispers of the ocean; gather enough, and they will fill the deepest chalice.

---

自己害自己，莫過於亂發脾氣。
The fiercest storm we face is the one we unleash upon ourselves in anger.

---

甘願做，歡喜受。
Offer your hands with a willing spirit, and receive life's echoes with a joyful heart.

---

能忍而不計較，則再大的困難也能克服。
When patience flows freely, unmeasured and unbound, even the highest walls of hardship will quietly crumble.

---

知識要用心體會，才能變成自己的智慧。
Words remain but echoes until they are embraced by the heart, where they quietly blossom into wisdom.

---

每天無所事事，是人生的消費者，積極有用，才是人生的創造者。
To drift through the days is merely to consume time; to serve with purpose is to weave the very fabric of life.

---

人生如果不能忍辱，就無法成就事業、學業與道德。
The soil of success and moral grace is often watered by the quiet endurance of life's passing indignities.

---

我們最強的對手不一定是別人，而可能是我們自己。
The grandest battles we fight are rarely with the world, but with the quiet shadows within our own minds.

---

想成就事業，就先成就自己的品德，昇華自己的人格。
Before reaching for the stars of success, first tend to the garden of your character; a noble spirit is the truest victory.

---

顧好自己的心念，才能真正愛人。
True love for another spills only from a mind that has first learned to shelter its own thoughts in peace.

---

盡多少本分，就得多少本事。
With every burden gracefully carried, the shoulders of your soul grow stronger.

---

小事不做，大事難成。
The grandest tapestry is woven from the humblest threads; to dismiss the small is to forsake the great.

---

站在半路，比走到目標更辛苦。
To linger in the wasteland of halfway is a heavier burden than pushing forward into the light of the summit.

---

要用心、細心，不要操心、煩心。
Walk through life with a heart full of care and a mind fully present, leaving the heavy baggage of worry behind.
